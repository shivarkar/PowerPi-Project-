The Transaction E-Sale Analysis project aims to evaluate and analyze electronic sales transactions to identify trends, patterns, and key performance indicators (KPIs) that impact business performance. This project will focus on extracting insights from transaction data to improve decision-making, enhance customer experience, and optimize revenue streams.

Objectives:
Analyze sales trends over different periods (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly).
Identify customer buying behaviors and preferences.
Detect high-performing products and categories.
Evaluate transaction patterns, including peak sales times and seasonal fluctuations.
Assess the impact of discounts, promotions, and pricing strategies on sales.
Provide data-driven recommendations for business growth.
Scope:
Data collection from e-commerce platforms, POS systems, or transaction logs.
Processing and cleaning of raw transaction data.
Implementation of data visualization techniques to interpret sales performance.
Application of machine learning or statistical models to forecast future sales.
Reporting and dashboard development for real-time tracking.
Expected Outcomes:
Improved sales forecasting and demand planning.
Enhanced understanding of customer behavior for targeted marketing.
Identification of key areas for business optimization.
Data-driven insights to increase profitability and efficiency.
